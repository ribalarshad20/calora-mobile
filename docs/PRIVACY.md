# Calora Privacy Policy (personal / portfolio)

Calora stores account credentials (hashed on the server), nutrition diary data, optional weight/water/activity sync, and AI prompts you submit.

- Passwords are hashed with Argon2id; API keys for Groq/Gemini never ship inside the app.
- Food barcode lookups use Open Food Facts.
- Step data is read on-device via Health Connect or sensors and synced to your account.
- We do not sell personal data. For a private friend group, access may be gated by invite code.

Contact the app operator for deletion requests.
